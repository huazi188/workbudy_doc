# 2026-10-20 个人创业观察

> **总览**
> **中文**：国内方面，一人公司仍是"能干活的人靠 AI 补上团队位"的形态，真正跑出收入的样本集中在窄场景工具与合规刚需，同期也出现"上过首页推荐、月入三千"的冷水案例。国外方面，独立开发者生态进入"收入可验证"阶段，TrustMRR 这类接 Stripe 只读 API 的验证平台把"截图造假"这条路堵死，头部与长尾的差距被摆到台面上。
> **English**: In China, the one-person company is still mostly "a capable operator using AI to cover the roles a team would fill"; the cases that actually reach revenue sit in narrow-scenario tools and compliance-driven must-haves, alongside cold-water examples like an App Store-featured app earning roughly RMB 3,000 a month. Internationally, indie hacking has entered a verification era — TrustMRR and similar platforms pull read-only Stripe keys and publish hourly-refreshed MRR, which closes off screenshot inflation and puts the gap between the top and the long tail on public display.
> **整体判断**：两类失败模式高度重复——"上线即完事"（没有分发）和"买量即收入"（停投即休克）。真正稳住的样本，共同点是先有一个不得不付费的窄场景，再谈增长。
> **English**: Two failure modes repeat across both regions: shipping without distribution, and buying growth that collapses the moment ad spend stops. The cases that hold share one trait — a narrow scenario where payment is not optional, addressed before growth is attempted.

## 一、收入里程碑 / Revenue Milestones

### 1. 公文排版插件十二年，年收入仅五六万 / A Twelve-Year Document Plugin That Earns Roughly RMB 50–60K a Year 【中国·1人】

**中文**：一个安装包只有 2.84MB 的 Word/WPS 公文排版插件，靠"赞赏制"活了十二年，年收入约 5–6 万元。它解决的是 GB/T 9704 国标排版问题——用户是"必须做对"而非"想要更快"，格式错一处就可能被退文问责，因此付费意愿和口碑传播都强，但赞赏制本身就是离散、不可预测的现金流，撑不起前置投入，也养不起销售团队，形成"收入天花板决定能力天花板"的闭环。（[虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4897815.html)）

**English**: A Word/WPS plugin for Chinese official-document formatting — a 2.84 MB installer — has survived twelve years on voluntary tips, earning roughly RMB 50,000–60,000 a year. It solves GB/T 9704 national-standard layout, which puts users in a "must get it right" position rather than a "want it faster" one: a single formatting error can get a document bounced or the clerk held accountable. That drives both willingness to pay and word of mouth. But a tip jar is discrete, unpredictable cash flow; it cannot fund upfront investment or a sales team, which locks the product into a loop where the revenue ceiling sets the capability ceiling. ([Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4897815.html))

### 2. 两人全职做"打工人小组件"，月均收入三千多 / A Two-Person Widget Team Averaging RMB 3,000 a Month 📉

**中文**：两位开发者（一名开发、一名设计）全职做"打工人小组件"，产品上过 App Store 首页推荐，也拿到了用户认可，但过去几个月平均月收入只有三千多元，两人五五分，每人到手一千多——报道原话是"这钱差不多只够点外卖的"。同一篇报道还指出"独立开发者三件套，记账、日记、待办事项，基本都不赚钱"。（[CSDN 转述腾讯新闻报道](https://blog.csdn.net/yangwenqqq/article/details/167465941)）

**English**: Two full-time developers — one building, one designing — shipped a "workplace widgets" app that landed an App Store homepage feature and genuine user approval. Average monthly revenue over recent months: about RMB 3,000, split 50/50, leaving each with a little over RMB 1,000. The report's own words: "that's about enough to cover takeout." The same piece notes that the indie developer's classic trio — expense tracking, journaling, to-dos — basically does not make money. ([CSDN, citing Tencent News](https://blog.csdn.net/yangwenqqq/article/details/167465941))

**English**: One operator's October 2026 line-by-line bill: seven tools across four categories, $365/month total, against roughly 16 hours a week saved at a conservative $75/hour. ## 四、失败与转身 / Failure and Pivot

### 1. VBA 技术栈的定时炸弹：重写要 6–12 个月，功能缩水三到五成 / The VBA Time Bomb: A 6–12 Month Rewrite, 30–50% Feature Loss 📉

**中文**：公文插件的十二年积累正撞上平台换代——微软已把 VBA 列为维护状态，WPS 转向 JS 框架，同一套代码的兼容性持续退化。评估结论是迁移到新架构需 6 至 12 个月全职重写，功能可能缩水 30%–50%，且成本远高于早期迁移，"技术债产生复利效应"。短期最致命的威胁不是技术栈，而是 WPS 官方原生集成——用户不需要装插件就解决了问题。（[虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4897815.html)）

**English**: Twelve years of accumulation runs straight into a platform change — Microsoft has put VBA into maintenance mode, WPS has moved to a JS framework, and the same codebase's compatibility keeps degrading. The assessment: migrating to the new architecture needs six to twelve months of full-time rewriting, features may shrink by 30–50%, and the cost far exceeds what an earlier migration would have been — technical debt compounds. The most lethal near-term threat is not the stack but WPS shipping native integration, which solves the problem for users without any plugin at all. ([Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4897815.html))

### 2. 上线 2 个月峰值 $8,843，停投后 30 天暴跌 90% / Peak $8,843 in Two Months, Then a 90% Collapse Once Ads Stopped 📉

**中文**：比利时独立开发者的情绪陪伴 iOS 应用 Pawchi（把用户的猫狗、玩偶扫描成像素伴侣），上线仅 2 个月跑出 $8,843 峰值流水，但近 30 天收入暴跌 90% 只剩 $302，挂牌售价从 3.2 万美元一路砍到 2.4 万美元。基建是 15 年老兵的配置（Flutter 自绘引擎、Laravel + AWS、PostHog 漏斗、AppsFlyer 归因、RevenueCat 内购，定价 $9.99/周与 $79.99/年）。死因两条：一是算力成本倒逼出硬锁变现——新手引导后强制弹 VIP 墙，不付费进不去，被大量一星差评骂"所谓 Free 完全不 Free"；二是伪留存与买量断奶——看板上仍挂 $863 MRR 和 184 个活跃订阅，实际是年费摊销撑起的账面存量，7、8 月的火爆依赖 Meta Ads，9 月改做零成本 UGC 传播，自然流量和付费转化瞬间归零。（[范师傅复盘](https://www.bestblogs.dev/status/2109229851542712452?entry=rss_article_item)）

**English**: Pawchi, a Belgian indie developer's emotional-companion iOS app that scans your pets, plushies, or figurines into a pixel companion, hit a peak of $8,843 in revenue within two months of launch — then dropped 90% over the following 30 days to $302, with the asking price cut from $32K to $24K. The infrastructure was a fifteen-year veteran's setup: Flutter for cross-platform rendering, Laravel on AWS, PostHog for funnel analysis, AppsFlyer for ad attribution, RevenueCat for IAP at $9.99/week and $79.99/year. Two causes of death. First, compute costs forced a hard paywall — a VIP wall fired right after onboarding, blocking anyone who would not pay, drawing one-star reviews saying "the so-called Free version isn't free at all." Second, fake retention plus ad dependency — the dashboard's $863 MRR and 184 active subscriptions were annual-plan amortization, not live cash, and when the developer swapped paid Meta traffic for zero-cost UGC in September, organic traffic and paid conversion went to zero overnight. ([Fan Shifu postmortem](https://www.bestblogs.dev/status/2109229851542712452?entry=rss_article_item))

## 二、产品与打法 / Product and Playbook

### 1. 单点功能被大厂一个开关覆盖：Huxe 融资 460 万美元后一年清盘 / A Single Feature Covered by One Toggle: Huxe Raised $4.6M and Cleared Out Inside a Year 📉

## 三、工具与工作流 / Tools and Workflows

### 1. 给"没有开发背景的操作者"的一套栈：贵在 Agent，其余全走免费额度 / A Stack Built for Operators Without a Dev Background: Pay for the Agent, Claim Credits for Everything Else

**中文**：一份 2026 年的单人交付栈写得相当实在：第一层只花钱在编码 Agent 上，理由是"这是你全部的工程人力"——Claude Code 这类 Agentic CLI 干多文件改动、跑测试、做迁移、真调试；Cursor 这类 Agentic 编辑器适合想逐条看 diff 的人。第二层托管与数据库坚持"无聊胜过聪明"：Railway / Render / Vercel + Supabase 或 Neon，关键理由是主流工具意味着模型第一次就能写对配置，不会给冷门平台幻觉参数。第三层鉴权与支付：用平台的 Supabase Auth，绝不让自己手写 session；支付只用 Stripe。第四层 AI 功能按难度路由——日常调用走便宜的开源权重端点，少数难题才上前沿模型。第五层，每一层都去领创业额度，"第一年这套栈基本不花钱"。（[Perkstack](https://perkstack.co/blog/vibe-coding-stack)）

**English**: A 2026 solo shipping stack laid out plainly. Layer one is the only place to spend real money, on the coding agent, on the reasoning that it is your entire engineering payroll — agentic CLI tools like Claude Code do multi-file changes, test runs, migrations, and real debugging loops, while agentic editors like Cursor suit people who want to steer every diff. Layer two, hosting and database, goes "boring beats clever": Railway, Render, or Vercel with Supabase or Neon, and the underrated reason is agent-friendliness — mainstream tools mean the model writes correct config on the first try instead of hallucinating flags for something exotic. Layer three, auth and payments: use the platform's built-in Supabase Auth rather than letting the agent hand-roll session handling, and Stripe for billing. Layer four routes model calls by difficulty — a cheap open-weight endpoint for routine work, a frontier model for the few hard ones. Layer five: every layer has a credit program attached, and working the checklist once realistically covers year one at no cash cost. ([Perkstack](https://perkstack.co/blog/vibe-coding-stack))

### 2. 单人 SaaS 的真实留存：$1M ARR 只剩 $559K / What a Solo SaaS Actually Keeps: $559K of a $1M ARR

**中文**：一篇 2026 年的单人 SaaS 测算把"ARR 幻觉"拆开算：$1M ARR 规模下，基础设施与分析工具吃掉收入的 4–7%，加上支付通道费和基础设施，到手约 $890K–$920K 毛额；再扣流失拖累与联邦加州税，美国单人 SaaS 实际留存约 $559K。同一模型下，印度单人创始人通过私人有限公司结构、约 25% 企业税率与更低基建消耗，留存接近 $680K。作者自己的账更直观：跨过 $5K MRR 时以为能落 $5K，扣掉 Razorpay 手续费、退款、GST 预留、托管和三份必需订阅（分析、邮件、错误追踪），银行到账约 $3,800——24% 的漏损。流失输入取自 ChartMogul 2025 年 B2B 净收入留存中位数 82%。（[maketocreate](https://maketocreate.com/one-person-saas-to-1m-arr-2026-solo-founder-playbook)）

**English**: A 2026 solo-SaaS model takes the ARR illusion apart. At $1M ARR, infrastructure and analytics tools eat 4–7% of revenue; after processor fees and infra, gross lands around $890K–$920K. The author's own books make it concrete: crossing $5K MRR, he assumed ~$5K take-home, but after Razorpay fees, refunds, a GST set-aside, hosting, and three required subscriptions (analytics, email, error tracking), cash in bank was ~$3,800 — a 24% leak. The churn input comes from ChartMogul's 2025 median B2B net revenue retention of 82%. ([maketocreate](https://maketocreate.com/one-person-saas-to-1m-arr-2026-solo-founder-playbook))

## 观察与提示

**中文**
1. **"上线即完成"是国内样本最集中的死因。** 那个连续 37 天零下载的案例里，作者自己的诊断是"我之前的推广内容全是'我做了个什么'的产品自述，那是空房间演讲"。App Store 首页推荐能带来一波下载，但带不来持续收入——"记账、日记、待办"三件套基本不赚钱这句话，值得每个想做通用工具的人抄在墙上。
2. **"能验证的收入"正在变成基础设施。** TrustMRR 这类接 Stripe / RevenueCat / App Store 只读密钥、每小时刷新的平台，让"贴截图报 MRR"失效。这对写复盘的人是好事——真实数字比漂亮数字有用；对打算卖项目的人也是压力——虚高的数字在尽调时会被打回原形。
3. **窄场景 + 不得不付费，是唯一在两个地区都成立的共性。** 公文排版（格式错就被退文问责）、菲律宾的记账应用（离线优先、不收集数据）、PostBridge（解决自己发帖的痛），共同点都不是"AI 很强"，而是"这件事不做不行"。
4. **买量是借款，不是收入。** Pawchi 从 $8,843 峰值掉到 $302 只用了 30 天，问题不在产品，在于 7、8 月的增长完全由 Meta Ads 提供。停投那一刻，真实的需求水位才露出来——而那个水位是零。
5. **公开的 kill line 是单人创业者最便宜的风控工具。** FacelessForge 的整堂课成本是 7 天和 $0，因为它提前把"什么时候放弃"写成了一条带日期的二元承诺。沉没成本是人性的默认设置，把它改成一个排好期的会议，比靠意志力扛要可靠得多。

**English**
1. **"Shipped" is the most common cause of death in the China sample.** In the 37-days-zero-downloads case, the author's own diagnosis was that all his promotion had been product monologue — "here's what I built" — which he calls speaking in an empty room. An App Store homepage feature buys a download spike, not recurring revenue; the line that the expense/journal/to-do trio "basically does not make money" deserves to be taped to the wall of anyone planning a general-purpose tool.
2. **Verified revenue is becoming infrastructure.** Platforms that pull read-only Stripe, RevenueCat, or App Store keys and refresh hourly have made screenshot-MRR obsolete. That is good news for anyone writing an honest postmortem — real numbers are more useful than flattering ones — and pressure for anyone selling a project, since inflated figures get taken apart in due diligence.
3. **Narrow scenario plus non-optional payment is the one trait that holds in both regions.** Official-document formatting (a formatting error gets a document bounced and the clerk held accountable), the Filipino budgeting app (offline-first, no data collection), PostBridge (solving the founder's own posting pain) — none of these succeed because "the AI is strong." They succeed because the job cannot be skipped.
4. **Paid acquisition is a loan, not revenue.** Pawchi fell from an $8,843 peak to $302 in thirty days. The product was not the problem; July and August growth was entirely Meta Ads. The moment spending stopped, the real demand level showed — and it was zero.
5. **A public kill line is the cheapest risk control a solo founder has.** FacelessForge's whole lesson cost seven days and $0, because the decision to quit had been written in advance as a dated, binary commitment. Sunk-cost bias is the human default; converting it into a scheduled meeting is more reliable than willpower.
