## 附：今天这组数字值得对一遍

**中文**
1. **同一份财报里，"AI 效益"和"AI 裁员"可以同时成立。** 甲骨文营收增 17%、云增 39%、RPO 增 325%，同时裁掉 2.1 万人。真正的原因不是 AI 让活变少了，是 AI 数据中心的资本支出（557 亿美元）超过了经营现金流——AI 既是裁员的理由，也是烧钱的理由，两者指向同一笔账。读财报时把这两条分开看，不容易被"AI 替代人力"这个叙事带偏。
2. **中国和美国大企业算的是两种账。** 河南移动算的是"一线员工自己搭应用"——37 人、47 项、16 个专业，成本趋近于零，效率提升 85%；美国银行算的是"130 个项目、每个都有回本测算、今年 2.5–3 亿美元"。前者在扩散能力，后者在核算回报。两条路都不错，但混着用容易两头不落。
3. **德国电信那张表值得当作模板。** 固定基准年（2023）、成本与收入分列、财务指标下面挂运营指标（来电降 55%、接触量占比 40%、响应 1 分钟）、并且说清省下来的钱去哪了（再投入德国光纤）。四件事做到，AI 汇报就不再是"我们很重视 AI"。
4. **ABB 那句"我不能给你一个 ABB 快了 18% 的数字"其实是最专业的回答。** 按职能分别测算、连 token 成本一起管，比一个好看的总体百分比有用得多。同一场电话会里，阳狮给出的是"自动化 25% 重复性任务"这种可执行的目标、而不是已实现结果——两种表述都要学会分辨。

**English**
1. **In a single filing, "AI benefit" and "AI layoffs" can both be true.** Oracle grew revenue 17%, cloud 39%, and RPO 325%, while cutting 21,000 jobs. The real cause was not AI making less work exist but AI data-centre capex ($55.7 billion) exceeding operating cash flow — AI is simultaneously the stated reason for the cuts and the reason the money is short, and both point at the same ledger line. Reading those two items separately keeps you from being swept along by the "AI replaces labour" narrative.
2. **Chinese and American enterprises are running two different ledgers.** China Mobile Henan counts diffusion — 37 people, 47 apps, 16 disciplines, near-zero cost, 85% efficiency gains. Bank of America counts returns — 130 projects, each with a defined payback, $250–300 million this year. Both are sound, but mixing them usually lands nowhere.
3. **Deutsche Telekom's table is worth using as a template.** A fixed baseline year (2023), cost and revenue reported on separate lines, operational metrics sitting beneath the financial ones (calls down 55%, 40% of contacts handled, one-minute response), and a stated destination for the savings (reinvested in German fibre). Do those four and an AI briefing stops being "we take AI very seriously."
4. **ABB's "I cannot give you a number — this has made ABB like 18% faster" is actually the most professional answer.** Measuring function by function and tracking token costs alongside is far more useful than one flattering aggregate. In the same batch of calls, Publicis offered "automate 25% of repetitive tasks" — an executable target rather than a realised result. Learning to tell those two apart is the skill.
