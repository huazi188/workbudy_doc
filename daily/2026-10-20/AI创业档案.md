## 二、产品与模式 / Product & Model

### 1. Dream-1：用「末端位姿」统一接口，绕开本体绑定 【美国·种子轮】

**中文**：Dream-1 基于「世界动作模型」（WAM），与传统机器人控制模型输出关节角度不同，它预测的是末端执行器位姿——夹爪去哪、开还是关。任何配备双臂平行夹爪的机器人共享这一接口维度，各自自带的逆运动学求解器把位姿转成关节指令。模型用人类日常任务的视频训练，看摄像头画面预测下一步动作。公司称对带夹爪的机器人可「零样本」运行；对合作方会进一步针对具体本体调优。目前能力集中在桌面任务：抓取、放置、叠衣服。**三个产品形态**：Dream API（发图像+状态+自然语言，回电机指令）、Fine-tuning as a Service（上传少量示范，数小时返回定制模型）、Robot Playground（浏览器远程操纵旧金山办公室真机）。

**English**: Dream-1 is built on a World Action Model: rather than joint angles, it predicts end-effector poses — where the gripper goes and whether it opens or closes. Any dual-arm parallel-jaw robot shares that interface, with each body's inverse kinematics converting poses to joint commands. Trained on video of everyday human tasks, it watches a camera feed and predicts the next action. The company claims zero-shot operation on gripper-equipped robots, with per-body tuning for partners. Current skills are desktop-level: picking, placing, folding laundry. Three products ship alongside: Dream API, fine-tuning as a service, and a browser-based Robot Playground that drives a real robot in San Francisco.

来源：[AI Parabellum](https://aiparabellum.com/dream-labs-90m-robot-ai) / [BT财经](https://businesstimescn.com/articles/630363.html)

### 2. 从 30 分钟数据到零样本泛化：DreamZero 的技术起点 【美国·种子轮】

**中文**：团队 2026 年 2 月还在英伟达时发布 DreamZero 研究，首次提出「世界动作模型」概念，用仅 30 分钟 YAM 机器人数据做后训练，就实现了零样本泛化到未见过任务的效果。CTO 葛云皓称：「如果 30 分钟就够了，那么『没数据就没用、没用就没数据』的死循环就可能被打破。」这是他们决定创业的触发点。当前全球工厂运行约 500 万台工业机器人，几乎每台只为单一任务编程，学到的东西无法迁移。

**English**: While still at Nvidia in February 2026, the team published DreamZero, introducing the World Action Model concept and showing zero-shot generalization to unseen tasks after post-training on just 30 minutes of YAM robot data. CTO Ge Yunhao says that if 30 minutes suffices, the "no data means no use, no use means no data" deadlock can break — the trigger for founding the company. Of the roughly 5 million industrial robots running worldwide, nearly all are programmed for a single task and cannot transfer what they learn.

来源：[BT财经](https://businesstimescn.com/articles/630363.html)
