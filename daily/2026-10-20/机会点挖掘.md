**一句话总览**：今天最值得动手的是**「AI 硬件落地的最后一公里交付」**——14 个案例证明这门生意真的在收钱（死鸡识别、汽配质检、连锁巡店都跑出了可量化回报），但每一个案例都靠一个本地方案商现场蹲点才成立；设备厂商不会蹲，互联网公司不肯蹲，这个"必须有人蹲在猪场和纺织厂里"的空位，就是机会。

**Today's most actionable opening is the last-mile delivery layer for deployed AI hardware** — 14 cases prove the money is real (dead-poultry detection, auto-parts inspection, chain-store audits, all with quantified returns), yet every one of them required a local integrator on site. Hardware vendors won't do it; internet companies won't do it. That gap is the opportunity.

## 第一类：创业类机会 / Startup Opportunities

### 1. AI 硬件落地「现场交付」服务队：按效果收费，不卖设备卖结果 【工业视觉·5-20万/项目·2-4个月回本】

**中文**：

**① 机会是什么**
做一个**下沉到工厂与农场现场的交付服务队**：客户不买设备、不买软件，只买"漏检率降到 X% / 巡检工时降到 Y 小时"这个结果。你负责选型（相机、边缘盒子、机器人）、部署、调参、陪跑，直到指标达标。收的是部署费 + 月度效果费。

This is a **field delivery crew that goes into factories and farms**: the customer buys neither hardware nor software — only the outcome (leak rate below X%, inspection hours below Y). You handle selection, deployment, tuning and hand-holding until the metric is met, charging a setup fee plus monthly performance fee.

**② 证据在哪**
- 据《AI硬件落地应用》第 3 条：**汽配厂李厂长：3 台工业相机 + 一个边缘盒子，花了 4 万多元，3 个月质检效率提升 40%、漏检率降到 1.1%**。这个数字最关键——4 万元投入、3 个月见效，对一家中小汽配厂是"敢花的钱"，说明付费意愿真实存在
- 据《AI硬件落地应用》第 1 条：姜堰蛋鸡场，一个鸡舍巡检从 4 小时压缩到 1 小时，10 万羽一批省 10 万元。**省下的钱是可算的**
- 据《AI硬件落地应用》第 5 条：远元集团近万家门店，把修脚服务流程变成 AI 视觉检查项——**同一套视觉能力横跨汽配、养殖、服务连锁三个行业**，说明底层能力通用，稀缺的是"能进现场把它调对的人"
- 据《AI硬件落地应用》第 4 条：茶百道 6500 家门店 + 廊坊华联超市，靠"摄像头利旧接入"做到问题检出率 80%、整改率 95%——**利旧**是这条生意的核心成本优势
- 判断：**14 个案例没有一条是"厂商直销成功"的，全部隐含一个本地方案商角色**。设备厂商（海康、大华）卖盒子不管调参；互联网公司（阿里、腾讯工业大脑）做平台不进猪场。中间这层是空的

**③ 从哪切进去**
- 第一步：**只做一个行业的一种检测**。建议从"农产品初加工分选"或"汽配外观件"里选一个——两者的共同点是：人工质检贵、标准客观、漏检后果可量化
- 硬件配置照抄案例里的低成本组合：3 台工业相机 + 边缘盒子，控制在 4-5 万元成本，报价 8-12 万
- 需要什么：1 个懂视觉算法的人 + 1 个愿意常年跑现场的销售/实施（这个人比算法重要）；第一单建议自己亲自蹲点
- 第一个付费客户从哪来：产业带的行业协会、地方工信局的"数字化改造"名单、以及那些已经买了相机但"用不起来"的厂（**这类客户最好谈，他们已经沉没了成本**）
- 多久能验证：2 个月内签下第一单，4 个月内交付并拿到效果确认

**风险与门槛**：这是**人力密集型的脏活**，无法靠软件规模化；每单都要重新调参，第 10 单不会比第 1 单轻松多少。真正的护城河只有"某个行业的检测经验库"——同一行业的第二单、第三单成本才会掉下来。**必须先在一个行业做够 5 单再谈扩张。**

**English**:
**① The opportunity** — A field delivery crew that goes on site at factories and farms. The customer buys an outcome, not equipment: leak rate under X%, inspection hours under Y. You handle hardware selection, deployment, tuning and hand-holding until the metric is hit, charging a setup fee plus a monthly performance fee.
**② Evidence** — Case 3: an auto-parts factory spent about RMB 40,000 on three industrial cameras and an edge box and saw inspection efficiency up 40% with leak rate at 1.1% within three months — small enough to approve, and it works. Case 1: a layer farm cut per-house inspection from four hours to one, saving RMB 100,000 per 100,000-bird batch. Case 5: Yuanyuan Group turned its foot-care service flow into AI visual checks across nearly 10,000 stores, proving the underlying capability spans auto parts, farming and service chains — what's scarce is someone who will go on site and tune it. Case 4: Chabaidao and a Langfang supermarket hit 80% detection and 95% remediation by reusing existing cameras, the key cost advantage. None of the 14 cases is a vendor direct sale; every one implies a local integrator that vendors won't staff and platforms won't send.
**③ How to start** — Pick one vertical and one inspection type: agricultural sorting or auto-parts cosmetic inspection, both with expensive manual labor, objective standards and quantifiable miss costs. Copy the low-cost stack (three cameras plus edge box, RMB 40–50K cost, quote RMB 80–120K). One vision engineer plus one person willing to live on site — the latter matters more. First customers: industry associations, local digitalization subsidy lists, and factories that already bought cameras and can't use them. Validate: first contract within two months, delivery and outcome confirmation within four.

### 2. 给「做不下去的独立开发者」做退出服务：收项目、翻新、再上架 【并购/翻新·3-15万·3-6个月】

**中文**：

**① 机会是什么**
做一个小型的**数字资产翻新商**：低价收购那些"有产品、有用户、没收入"的独立开发项目（App、插件、小工具），做三件事——修漏斗、换定价、补分发，然后要么自己运营、要么转手卖给有流量的人。

A small **digital asset refurbisher**: buy distressed indie projects at a low price — products with users but no revenue — then fix the funnel, reprice and rebuild distribution, and either operate them or resell to someone with traffic.

**② 证据在哪**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 20 条：Pawchi，峰值 $8,843 流水，30 天暴跌 90% 到 $302，**挂牌价从 3.2 万美元一路砍到 2.4 万美元**——这是明确的"卖方在降价、急售"信号
- 据《个人创业观察》第 25 条：CodeParrot，两年半做到 $1,500 MRR，融资 50 万美元后关停。复盘原话是"一个带 Stripe 账户的原型，不是公司"——**这类项目有产品、有品牌、有代码，缺的只是需求和分发**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 8 条：连续 37 天零下载的项目，作者自己做了四件事就把 GitHub 日均克隆从 14 拉到 178（**10-13 单日 187，10 月 1-13 日合计 551**）——**证明"翻新"这个动作是真的有效的，而且成本极低**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 22 条：FacelessForge 7 天 0 付费就关停，成本只有 7 天和 $0——**说明市场上有大量"便宜到几乎白送"的项目在流动**
- 判断：**卖方在降价、翻新手法已被验证、收购成本接近于零，三个条件同时成立时，中间商才有位置**

**③ 从哪切进去**
- 第一步：**不做平台，先自己收 1 个**。去 Acquire.com、MicroAcquire、Indie Hackers 的 for-sale 板块，专门找"上线 6-18 个月、有 100+ 用户、MRR 低于 $500"的项目，出价 $2,000-5,000
- 翻新三板斧照抄 37 天那个案例：**①把价格从"一次买断"改成"低门槛订阅 + 限时降价款" ②把推广内容从"我做了什么"改成 building-in-public 按周对账 ③把工程能力打包成咨询做第二条收入线**
- 需要什么：1 个人 + 每月 $200 的推广预算；不需要开发能力，需要**分发能力**
- 第一个客户从哪来：这个生意没有"客户"，只有"标的"和"买家"。买家是那些有流量但没产品的人（垂直社群主、YouTuber、工具站站长）
- 多久能验证：收第 1 个项目 3 个月内转手，赚到第一笔差价（哪怕只有 5,000 元）

**风险与门槛**：**这是资产生意不是产品生意**，现金流靠周转，一旦某个项目砸手里就吃掉前三个的利润。另外"买家"极难找，多数翻新项目的真实出路不是转手，而是自己运营到能覆盖成本——那就变成"一个人运营 N 个小产品"，反而是最累的形态。

**English**:
**① The opportunity** — A digital asset refurbisher: buy distressed indie projects cheaply — products with users but no revenue — then fix the funnel, reprice and rebuild distribution, and operate or resell them. **② Evidence** — Pawchi peaked at $8,843 then fell 90% in 30 days to $302, with the asking price cut from $32K to $24K: sellers are discounting. CodeParrot reached only $1,500 MRR in two and a half years after raising $500K and shut down, its own postmortem calling it "a prototype with a Stripe account, not a company." The 37-days-zero-download case shows refurbishment works and costs almost nothing: four moves took daily GitHub clones from 14 to 178 (187 on October 13; 551 for October 1–13). FacelessForge shut down after seven days at zero cost, showing how cheaply such projects change hands. Discounting sellers, a validated playbook and near-zero acquisition cost all hold at once. **③ How to start** — Don't build a platform; buy one. Hunt Acquire.com, MicroAcquire and Indie Hackers listings for projects live 6–18 months with 100+ users and under $500 MRR, bidding $2,000–5,000. Apply the three moves: shift one-time pricing to low-barrier subscription with a limited-time discount; replace "look what I built" with weekly building-in-public accounting; package engineering capability into consulting as a second line. One person, $200/month promotion budget, distribution skill rather than dev skill. Validate: flip the first project within three months for any margin.

### 3. 给传统软件做「Agent 接口层」改造：把 20 年老系统接进模型 【系统集成·5-15万/项目·2-3个月】

**中文**：

**① 机会是什么**
大量中小企业的核心系统是 10-20 年前的（VBA、Delphi、老版 ERP、Excel 宏）。这些系统不会消失，但也接不进任何 AI。做一层**"Agent 接口层"**：把这些系统的功能包成命令行或 MCP 工具，让模型能调用。

A layer that turns two-decade-old business systems — VBA, Delphi, legacy ERP, Excel macros — into callable tools: CLI commands or MCP endpoints that a model can invoke. These systems aren't going away, and today they connect to nothing.

**② 证据在哪**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 18 条：**VBA 技术栈被直接称为"定时炸弹"——重写要 6-12 个月，功能缩水三到五成**。这意味着"重写"这条路在经济上是不通的，客户只能选"包一层"
- 据《个人创业观察》第 7 条：一位开发者的做法被写进日报——**把插件改成"AI 用的工具"，9 个命令行开关做 Agent 接口**。这是已验证的最小可行路径
- 据《个人创业观察》第 1 条：一个公文排版插件活了十二年、年收入五六万，说明**这类老系统的存量极大且极度稳定**
- 据《Agent落地方案》第 1 条：售后工单 Agent 的四类工具（`order_query` / `ticket_history_search` / `knowledge_search` / `customer_info_query`）本质就是**给老系统包接口**，只是做的人没意识到这是独立生意
- 判断：**"重写成本 6-12 个月 + 缩水三到五成" vs "包接口 2-3 个月"**——这个价差本身就是商业模式

**③ 从哪切进去**
- 第一步：只接三类最高频操作——**查（订单/库存/客户）、写（建单/改状态）、报（导出 Excel）**。不碰计算逻辑，不碰权限改造
- 技术方案照抄：命令行包装 + JSON 输入输出 + 一个 MCP server 收口。**不改原系统一行代码**
- 需要什么：1 个懂老系统（VBA/SQL Server/Oracle）的人 + 1 个懂 MCP 的人，两个人两周能出第一个 demo
- 第一个付费客户从哪来：**已经在用某个 AI 工具但抱怨"它读不到我们系统数据"的企业**。这类客户不用教育，他们已经在找解决方案了
- 多久能验证：2 个月内签第一单

**风险与门槛**：**每个客户的系统都不一样，没有规模效应**，第 10 单和第 1 单一样累。唯一的解法是把接口层做成**可配置的适配器库**（按数据库类型、按 ERP 品牌分），做到第 10 单时有 70% 能复用。另外老系统的技术负责人往往就是"既得利益者"，改造可能触动他的位置，要有心理准备。

**English**:
**① The opportunity** — A layer that exposes two-decade-old business systems — VBA, Delphi, legacy ERP, Excel macros — as callable tools (CLI or MCP) so a model can use them. These systems won't disappear and today connect to nothing. **② Evidence** — The VBA stack is described as a time bomb: a rewrite takes 6–12 months and loses 30–50% of functionality, so rewriting is economically impossible and wrapping is the only option. One developer's approach is already documented: turn the plugin into "a tool for AI" with nine CLI switches as the agent interface — a validated minimal path. A document-formatting plugin has survived twelve years on RMB 50–60K a year, showing how large and stable this legacy base is. The four tool classes in the after-sales ticket agent are, in essence, wrappers around legacy systems — the builders just never noticed it was a standalone business. **③ How to start** — Wrap only three operation classes: read (orders, inventory, customers), write (create, change status), report (export). Don't touch calculation logic or permissions. CLI wrapper plus JSON in/out behind one MCP server, zero changes to the original system. One legacy-stack person plus one MCP person ships a demo in two weeks. First customers: firms already using an AI tool that complains it can't read their system data — no education needed. Validate: first contract in two months.

### 4. 「不上首页也能卖」的分发代运营：给小工具做冷启动 【增长服务·3-8千/月·1个月见效】

**中文**：

**① 机会是什么**
专门服务"产品做完了、卖不出去"的独立开发者，做分发代运营：不碰产品，只负责让第一批 100 个用户出现。收费是月度服务费 + 效果分成。

Distribution-as-a-service for indie developers who finished the product and can't sell it: you don't touch the product, you just make the first 100 users appear. Monthly retainer plus performance share.

**② 证据在哪**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 2 条：两人全职做"打工人小组件"，**上过 App Store 首页推荐，月均收入仍只有三千多**，两人五五分每人到手一千多。报道原话"这钱差不多只够点外卖的"——**上首页都救不了，说明分发能力是独立于产品质量的独立变量**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 1 条：公文排版插件十二年，年收入五六万——**产品极稳、增长为零**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 6 条：独立开发者"三件套"（记账、日记、待办）基本都不赚钱——**最拥挤的品类，最缺分发**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 8 条：37 天零下载的项目，作者自己复盘说"我之前的推广内容全是'我做了个什么'的产品自述，**那是空房间演讲**"——问题诊断已经写在明面上了
- 据《个人创业观察》第 23 条：菲律宾三位开发者几周做完、无融资、靠应用商店直销 + 社区自然传播就登顶——**证明分发路径存在，只是大部分开发者不会走**
- 判断：**大量开发者的自我诊断是"我不会推广"而不是"我产品不行"**，这类客户的教育成本几乎为零

**③ 从哪切进去**
- 第一步：**只接 3 个客户，且必须是自己用过的产品**。做一个标准化的 30 天冷启动包：第 1 周改落地页与定价、第 2 周铺 5 个垂直社区、第 3 周做 building-in-public 连载、第 4 周复盘并给下一阶段建议
- 报价策略：**低价起步 + 效果分成**（比如月费 3000 + 新增付费用户首月收入的 30%）。让客户的风险最小
- 需要什么：1 个人，会写能让人点进去的文案；不需要投广告预算
- 第一个付费客户从哪来：Indie Hackers、V2EX、即刻、小红书上的"求推广建议"帖。**这些人是自己举手求救的**
- 多久能验证：1 个月内第一个客户续费，就说明这条路成立

**风险与门槛**：**效果难以归因**——用户涨了到底是你的功劳还是恰好赶上推荐位，客户会质疑；且这个生意的天花板明显（一个月最多服务 5-8 个客户）。护城河只能是"我手上有 20 个垂直社区的入口资源"，否则随时被更便宜的人替代。

**English**:
**① The opportunity** — Distribution-as-a-service for indie developers who have a finished product and no sales. You don't touch the product; you make the first 100 users appear. Monthly retainer plus performance share. **② Evidence** — A two-person widget team landed App Store front-page featuring and still averaged RMB 3,000 a month, RMB 1,000 each — featuring doesn't save you, so distribution is a variable independent of product quality. A document plugin ran twelve years at RMB 50–60K annually: rock-solid product, zero growth. The indie trifecta of budgeting, journaling and to-do apps "basically doesn't make money" — the most crowded categories lack distribution most. The 37-days-zero-download author diagnosed himself: all his promotion was "look what I built," which he called "a speech in an empty room." Three Filipino developers shipped in weeks, raised nothing, and topped charts on store listing plus community spread — the path exists, most developers just don't walk it. **③ How to start** — Take three clients, only products you'd use. Ship a standard 30-day package: week 1 landing page and pricing, week 2 five vertical communities, week 3 building-in-public series, week 4 review and next-stage plan. Price low plus share: RMB 3,000 a month plus 30% of first-month revenue from new paying users. One person who can write copy that gets clicked; no ad budget. First customers from people openly asking for promotion help on Indie Hackers, V2EX, Jike and Xiaohongshu.

### 5. 「验证型」产品的第三方核数：给 MRR 造假潮做反证 【数据服务·按次收费·长期】

**中文**：

**① 机会是什么**
TrustMRR 这类平台把"贴截图报 MRR"这条路堵死了，但**中国开发者没有对应的东西**——Stripe 只读密钥、RevenueCat、App Store 的验证链路在国内基本走不通。做一套**面向国内独立开发者的收入核数服务**：接微信支付/支付宝商户号只读权限、接应用商店后台，出一份可核查的收入证明。

A revenue attestation service for Chinese indie developers: TrustMRR-style verification doesn't work here because Stripe read-only keys, RevenueCat and App Store verification are largely inaccessible domestically. Connect read-only WeChat Pay / Alipay merchant permissions and app store backends, and issue a checkable revenue statement.

**② 证据在哪**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 4 条总览：**"TrustMRR 这类接 Stripe 只读 API 的验证平台把'截图造假'这条路堵死，头部与长尾的差距被摆到台面上"**——需求侧的问题已经被验证过
- 据《个人创业观察》第 24 条：FacelessForge 的作者**拿竞品在 TrustMRR 上 $74K MRR 当需求验证**，然后自己 7 天 0 付费。他事后总结"竞品收入不是你这个 SKU 的需求验证"——**说明市场极度缺乏可信的横向对比数据**
- 据《个人创业观察》第 26 条：$1M ARR 实际只剩 $559K 的测算——**收入口径混乱到需要专门写文章拆解**

### 7. 供应链「隐性成本」体检：用 AI 硬件案例做报价工具 【制造业咨询·2-8万/次·1-2个月】
