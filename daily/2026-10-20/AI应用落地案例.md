## 附：今天这三条值得留意

**中文**
1. **评价标准的切换是本轮最实质的变化。** 国常会把"落地应用"和"现实生产力"提到前面，工信部同步给出 20 个细分领域、1000 余个场景的参照清单——这意味着"我们上线了 AI"不再算成绩，得说清落在哪条产线、良品率提了几个点。温州那场博览会上参展商的追问很说明问题："这条产线能不能改？改造周期多长？良品率能提几个点？"
2. **越是高危场景，智能体越只做"建议"不做"执行"。** 弘润石化的炼化模型输出的是阀门与温压调参**方案**，加氢裂化响应时间从 4 小时到 2 小时，但方案要人来执行；医院病理讲的是"AI 初筛 + 医师终审"双重质控。这两类场景共同点：错了代价太高，所以人在环不是过渡态，是终态。
3. **国际案例里最硬的数字来自"先建虚拟、再动实物"。** 西门子埃尔朗根 85% 的工程减量不是让 AI 写代码省出来的，是用数字孪生生成照片级 PCB 图像、让质检系统在实物原型出现之前就训练完成。这套逻辑对任何"试错成本高、实物打样慢"的制造业场景都成立。

**English**
1. **The shift in yardstick is the most substantive change this cycle.** The State Council put deployment and real productivity first, and MIIT supplied a reference list of 20 sub-sectors and over 1,000 scenarios — meaning "we shipped AI" no longer counts as an achievement; you have to say which line it landed on and how many points yield improved. The questions exhibitors asked at the Wenzhou expo say it plainly: can this line be converted, how long does conversion take, how many points of yield does it add?
2. **The higher the stakes, the more agents advise and the less they execute.** Hongrun's refining model outputs valve and temperature-pressure adjustment **plans**, cutting hydrocracking response from four hours to two, but a human executes them; hospital pathology describes "AI pre-screen plus physician final review." Both share a trait: mistakes are too expensive, so human-in-the-loop is the end state, not a transition.
3. **The hardest number internationally comes from building virtual first, then touching physical.** Erlangen's 85% engineering reduction did not come from AI writing code — it came from digital twins generating photorealistic PCB images so inspection systems finish training before a physical prototype exists. That logic transfers to any manufacturing setting where trial-and-error is expensive and physical sampling is slow.
