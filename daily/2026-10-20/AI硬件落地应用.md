### 3. 汽配厂李厂长：3 台工业相机加一个边缘盒子花了 4 万多，3 个月质检效率提 40%、漏检率 1.1% / Auto-Parts Plant: Three Cameras and an Edge Box for RMB 40K, 40% Inspection Efficiency Gain in Three Months 📈 【小厂质检·工业相机+边缘计算·年产值 8000 万厂】

**English**: At an auto-parts plant with RMB 80 million in annual output, plant manager Li ran the numbers and declined the imported AI inspection system quoted at RMB 380,000 with a two-month commissioning period — payback was too slow.
